# StudyMate Low Fidelity Wireframes

The importable visual source is `docs/design/assets/studymate-low-fidelity-wireframes.svg`. Import it into Figma as the starting point for the Chapter 4 wireframe page; preserve this file as the repository-side source of the initial layout.

## Frames

1. **Dashboard**: course list, one clear create-course action, and a recent study continuation area.
2. **Document library**: upload action plus a readable list of document processing states.
3. **Study session**: selected-document context, grounded chat, and citations directly below the answer.
4. **Quiz**: current question, progress, submit action, and source-aware feedback after submission.

## Figma refinement rules

- Convert repeated boxes into components only after their state and purpose are clear.
- Add mobile frames after the desktop information hierarchy is agreed.
- Do not remove empty, loading, processing, failed, or insufficient-context states during visual refinement.
- Link the final Figma frames in `FIGMA_LINKS.md` once a team member creates the file.
