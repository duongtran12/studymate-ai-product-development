# StudyMate Personas

## Primary persona: Minh, an undergraduate student

Minh studies several technical modules at once. Each module has slides, handouts, and lecture notes distributed across different channels. Before an exam, Minh spends too much time locating definitions and turning documents into revision questions.

### Goals

- Ask a question and receive an answer tied to the selected course material.
- Understand where an answer came from before trusting it.
- Create quick revision quizzes without manually rewriting all source content.

### Pain points

- Generic chatbots can answer plausibly but do not show whether a claim came from Minh's notes.
- Searching individual PDFs is slow and gives little help with synthesis.
- Manually creating flashcards and quizzes is repetitive.

### Success criteria

Minh can upload a supported document, ask a question within its scope, see a cited answer, and complete a generated quiz in one study session.

## Secondary persona: Lan, a study-group coordinator

Lan organizes a small study group and needs a predictable way to prepare questions from a shared set of materials. Lan values clear ownership and avoids exposing documents to users who were not given access.

### Goals

- Organize materials by course and study session.
- Revisit prior questions and generated quizzes.
- Trust that another account cannot access private study data.

### Success criteria

Lan can create a course workspace, return to an earlier session, and receives an access-denied result when attempting to open another user's private resource.

## Product implication

The MVP prioritizes evidence-backed answers, document organization, understandable processing status, and strict resource ownership over broad social sharing or advanced analytics.
